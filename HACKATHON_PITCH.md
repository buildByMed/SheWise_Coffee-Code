# SheWise - 60 Second Pitch

## The Problem
Women face unique health challenges but often lack judgment-free, trustworthy spaces to seek information, track symptoms, and find healthcare. Stigma, misinformation, and scattered resources make it harder for women to understand their bodies and make informed health decisions.

## The Solution
**SheWise** - A comprehensive women's health platform that empowers women to:
- Understand symptoms through intelligent questionnaires
- Track health patterns (cycle, sleep, mood, weight)
- Access verified health information
- Find local healthcare providers
- Get support in a judgment-free environment

## Key Features
1. **Symptom Questionnaire** - Smart multi-step form with adaptive questions
2. **Health Tracker** - Visual charts tracking cycle, sleep, mood, weight
3. **Supportive Chatbot** - Myth-busting, judgment-free health Q&A
4. **Knowledge Center** - Verified articles and health myths
5. **Hospital Locator** - Find women's health clinics in your area
6. **Privacy-First** - All data stored locally on user's device

## Market Opportunity
- **Target:** Women 18-50 in India (starting with Delhi)
- **Market Size:** 400M women in India seeking health info
- **Problem Severity:** High - stigma around women's health discussions
- **Addressable Market:** Growing digital health adoption in tier-2/3 cities

## Competitive Advantages

## Judge Questions Prep

**Q: Why women's health?**  
A: Women face unique health challenges with high stigma. Digital-first solutions can remove barriers to health information.

**Q: Why India/Delhi?**  
A: Large female population, growing digital adoption, underserved by healthcare tech, multiple language needs.

**Q: How do you plan to make money?**  
A: Freemium model, B2B partnerships with hospitals, corporate wellness programs.

**Q: What about competition (Flo, Clue)?**  
A: We're India-first, privacy-focused, and positioned for underserved markets. Plus focus on holistic health, not just menstrual tracking.

**Q: Why localStorage and not a real backend?**  
A: MVP strategy for hackathon. Production version will use PostgreSQL + Better Auth for secure cloud storage.

**Q: How will you get users?**  
A: Social media, healthcare partnerships, corporate wellness programs, word-of-mouth among women communities.

**Q: Data privacy concerns?**  
A: All data stays on device. No cloud collection yet. Clear privacy policy. Can opt-in to cloud sync later.
