# SheWise - Quick Reference Card

**Use this during your hackathon presentation for quick facts.**

---

## PRODUCT AT A GLANCE

**What:** Women's health empowerment platform  
**Where:** India-focused (starting Delhi)  
**Who:** Women 18-50  
**Why:** Safe, judgment-free space for health info  
**How:** Questionnaire, tracker, chat, knowledge base  

---

## THE PROBLEM

❌ Women face judgment discussing health  
❌ No centralized tracking for health patterns  
❌ Scattered, unreliable health information  
❌ Difficulty finding women-friendly doctors  
❌ Taboo around women's health in India  

---

## THE SOLUTION

✓ Private health tracking (data on device)  
✓ Judgment-free environment  
✓ Evidence-based information  
✓ Doctor finder by location  
✓ Multi-language support  
✓ Beautiful, intuitive UI  

---

## 6 CORE FEATURES

1. **Symptom Questionnaire** - 5-step adaptive form
2. **Health Tracker** - Log & visualize cycle, sleep, mood, weight
3. **Supportive Chatbot** - Myth-busting Q&A
4. **Knowledge Center** - Searchable health articles
5. **Doctor Locator** - Find women-friendly clinics

## HOW DATA WORKS

```
User → React State → localStorage → Persisted
       (all on device, no cloud)
```

Data persists after:
- ✓ Page refresh
- ✓ Browser restart
- ✓ Hours/days away
- Lost if:
- ✗ Browser cache cleared
- ✗ Private/incognito mode
- ✗ Different browser
- ✗ Different device

## THE PITCH

"Most women feel uncomfortable discussing health due to judgment and taboo. SheWise removes this barrier by providing a private, judgment-free space to track health patterns, learn from verified sources, and find supportive doctors. We're starting in India where 300M+ women need this solution. Unlike global apps, we're built for Indian context with local doctors, multiple languages, and cultural sensitivity. Our early metrics show strong engagement, and a clear path to profitability through freemium + partnerships."

## ANSWERS TO TOUGH QUESTIONS

**Q: Why would doctors work with you?**
A: Revenue share on consultations, patient referrals, brand building

**Q: How do you compete with Flo/Clue?**
A: Different market (India vs US), better privacy, holistic health focus

**Q: What about regulation (medical boards)?**
A: Non-diagnostic, educational only. Medical team for compliance.

**Q: How do you reach users?**
A: Social media, partnerships, word-of-mouth, corporate wellness

**Q: What if users don't trust tech for health?**
A: Educational content + testimonials + transparent privacy

**Q: Why not just go to hospital?**
A: Shame, cost, accessibility. We're complement, not replacement.

**Q: How will you make money initially?**
A: Partnerships + freemium. Patience for profitability.
