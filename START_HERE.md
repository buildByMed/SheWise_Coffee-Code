## 🎓 KNOW YOUR PRODUCT

### The Problem (30 seconds to explain)
Women face stigma discussing health, lack centralized tracking, can't find trustworthy info, feel judged by doctors. India specifically: 300M+ women underserved.

### The Solution (30 seconds to explain)
SheWise: Private (data on device), judgment-free (empathetic design), informative (verified sources), local (India-specific doctors), accessible (free forever tier).

### The Market (30 seconds to explain)
300M+ women in India. 50M+ in urban areas seeking health info. Digital health adoption accelerating. Underserved by Western apps (Flo/Clue). Growing corporate wellness market.

### The Tech (30 seconds to explain)
Modern stack: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. Browser-based with localStorage persistence. MVP ready, backend-ready architecture. Can deploy immediately.

### The Business (30 seconds to explain)
Multiple revenue streams: Freemium (free core, paid advanced), B2B2C (hospital partnerships), Telemedicine (commission on consultations), Corporate wellness programs.

## ❓ TOUGH QUESTIONS PREP

**Q: Why would a doctor work with you?**  
A: Revenue share, patient referrals, brand building, access to engaged user base

**Q: How do you compete with Flo/Clue?**  
A: We're India-first (not US-first), privacy-first (not cloud-first), holistic (not period-only)

**Q: What about regulation (medical boards)?**  
A: All content is educational only, clearly marked as non-diagnostic. Have medical advisors for compliance.

**Q: How will you get users?**  
A: Social media (organic reach), partnerships, corporate wellness, word-of-mouth, health influencers

**Q: What if users don't trust tech?**  
A: Education + testimonials + transparent privacy. We're complement to doctors, not replacement.
